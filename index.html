About

Fedor Ananin is a product leader with 15+ years building lending and banking products across fintech — at Sber, Alfa-Bank, VK, Modulbank and Zaman Bank. He has launched credit products for small businesses and consumers, including one of Russia's first one-hour online business loans, built product teams of 50+ people, and run more than 400 customer-development interviews.

He founded and led Virtual Marketer, an AI marketing startup for small businesses, and most recently directed a cross-border India–Russia platform for labor migrants at Zaman Bank and Nomadee. He operates at the CPO / VP Product level and works at the intersection of fintech, SME banking and applied AI.
